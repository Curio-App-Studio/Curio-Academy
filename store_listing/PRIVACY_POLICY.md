# Privacy Policy for Curio Academy

**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 23, 2026  

Curio Academy ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an interactive, kid-friendly educational application designed to help children (from Pre-K / LKG up to Grade 8) learn and practice Mathematics and English concepts. 

We take children's privacy and online safety very seriously. We strictly comply with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, and the **Google Play Families Policy**.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
Curio Academy is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture:
- **No Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, dates of birth, photos, or audio recordings.
- **No Account Registration:** Users do not need an account or login to access learning features in the application.
- **No Third-Party Analytics SDKs:** We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs that track user identity or device fingerprints.

---

### 2. Advertising & Premium Subscription Policy
To make high-quality educational content accessible to all families, Curio Academy offers both a free tier and an ad-free subscription tier:

- **Kid-Safe Contextual Advertising (Free Tier):**
  - Advertisements shown to young learners are strictly contextual and family-friendly.
  - In accordance with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA, all advertising providers are **Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs**.
  - We strictly **prohibit personalized or interest-based advertising**, behavioral remarketing, and ad-tracking cookies/identifiers.
- **Ad-Free Premium Subscription:**
  - Users have the option to subscribe to **Curio Academy Premium**.
  - The premium subscription completely removes all advertisements, providing an uninterrupted, 100% ad-free educational environment.

---

### 3. Local Device Storage Only
All user progress, stars, badges, class selections, and audio preferences are stored **locally on the user's device** using private application storage (`SharedPreferences`).
- None of this progress data is transmitted to external servers or cloud services.
- If the user clears the app data or uninstalls the App, this local progress information is permanently deleted from the device.

---

### 4. Device Permissions
Curio Academy requests minimal device permissions:
- **Audio Output:** Used solely to play educational sound effects and local Text-to-Speech (TTS) prompts to help younger children understand questions.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** The App does not request access to the microphone, camera, contacts, precise location, or device storage files.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)
Because Curio Academy is intended for children under the age of 13:
- We do not solicit or knowingly collect personal information from children.
- We do not include any links directing children to external social networks or unvetted web pages without a parental gate.
- Parents or legal guardians have full control over the application by managing local device access and app data.

---

### 6. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
