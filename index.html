Google Play Families Policy & COPPA Compliant

Privacy Policy for Curio Academy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026 | Last Updated: September 23, 2026

Curio Academy ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an interactive, kid-friendly educational application designed to help children (from Pre-K / LKG up to Grade 8) learn and practice Mathematics and English concepts.

We take children's privacy and online safety very seriously. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Curio Academy operates with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, dates of birth, photos, or voice recordings.
  • No Account Registration: Users do not need an account, password, or login to access core learning features in the application.
  • No Third-Party Analytics Trackers: We do not embed analytics SDKs that track user identity or device fingerprints.

2. Advertising & Premium Subscription Policy

To keep high-quality learning accessible to all children, Curio Academy provides a free tier alongside an optional ad-free premium subscription:

  • Kid-Safe Contextual Ads (Free Tier): Advertisements served in the free tier are strictly non-personalized, family-appropriate, and contextual. We strictly use Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs. Behavioral remarketing, personalized profiling, and tracking of children are strictly prohibited.
  • Ad-Free with Subscription: Families can choose to upgrade to a Curio Academy Premium subscription to completely remove all advertisements for an uninterrupted, distraction-free learning experience.

3. Local Device Storage Only

All user progress, stars, badges, class selections, and audio preferences are stored locally on the user's device using private application storage (SharedPreferences).

  • None of this progress data is transmitted to external servers or cloud services.
  • If the user clears application data or uninstalls the App, this local progress information is permanently deleted from the device.

4. Device Permissions

Curio Academy requests minimal device permissions:

  • Audio Output: Used solely to play educational sound effects and local Text-to-Speech (TTS) prompts to help younger children understand questions.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The App does not request access to the microphone, camera, contacts, precise location, or device storage files.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Because Curio Academy is intended for children under the age of 13:

  • We do not solicit or knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • We do not include links directing children to external social networks or unvetted web pages without a parental gate.
  • Parents or legal guardians have full control over the application by managing local device access and app data.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
